# Echoids

> Generated from the game code by `tools/gen_wiki.c`. Don't edit by hand: run the tool again after changing the game.

Everything about an echoid, apart from its level and XP, is worked out from a **MAC address**, so the same MAC always gives exactly the same echoid. Wild echoids come from the MAC (BSSID) of a WiFi access point, and your starter comes from your Flipper's own Bluetooth MAC.

## What the MAC decides

| Trait | Notes |
|---|---|
| Type | One of 8, all equally common ([Types](Types.md)) |
| Rarity | Common to Legendary, see below |
| Species | One of 3 per type; rarer echoids lean to the later species |
| Base stats | HP, ATK, DEF and SPD |
| Star rating | How good its random stat rolls are |
| Name | Generated by a Markov chain ([Types → Names](Types.md#names)) |
| Sprite | A 16×16 symmetric pixel creature |
| Moves | Its learnset, from its type and species ([Types and Moves](Types-and-Moves.md)) |
| Active hours | When it comes out each day, see [Time of day](#time-of-day) |

A wild echoid's **level** is the one thing the MAC doesn't decide. It's set near your lead's level, from 2 below to 2 above, plus 1 for Rare and rarer, so fights stay fair.

## Rarity

| Rarity | Badge | Chance | Stat bonus (HP / others) | Base catch rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | `C` | 50% | +0 / +0 | 60% |
| Uncommon | `U` | 28% | +3 / +2 | 45% |
| Rare | `R` | 14% | +6 / +4 | 30% |
| Epic | `E` (boxed) | 6% | +9 / +6 | 18% |
| Legendary | `L` (boxed) | 2% | +12 / +8 | 8% |

The badge letter is shown in the Collect list, the Team and PAC lists and in battle. Epic and Legendary badges are boxed so they stand out. See [Battles](Battles.md#catching) for how the catch rate is used.

## Stats

Every echoid has four stats. Its **base** stats are a fixed floor, plus a **random roll** from its MAC, plus its rarity bonus:

| Stat | Floor | Random roll | Rarity bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| HP | 24 | 0–16 | +3 per rarity step |
| ATK | 8 | 0–8 | +2 per rarity step |
| DEF | 8 | 0–8 | +2 per rarity step |
| SPD | 8 | 0–8 | +2 per rarity step |

Stats grow with level: `stat = base + base × (level − 1) / 8 + growth × (level − 1)`, where growth is 4 for HP and 1 for the others. **ATK** raises your damage, **DEF** lowers the damage you take, **SPD** decides who moves first and how easily you run away, and **HP** is how much damage the echoid can take.

## Star rating

The star rating judges only the **random rolls**, not rarity, so even a Common can be perfect.

| Stat stars | Roll |
|---|---|
| ★★★ | The highest possible roll: the stat can't be stronger |
| ★★☆ | The top half of the range |
| ★☆☆ | The bottom half |

The four stats' stars add up to between 4 and 12, giving the overall rating:

| Overall | Star total | How common |
|---|---|---|
| ★★★ **perfect** | 12: every stat ★★★ | 1 in 12393 |
| ★★☆ | 8–11 | 23.6% |
| ★☆☆ | 4–7 | 76.4% |

The overall stars appear in the Collect info panel and on the details screen. The details screen's **Rating** page (↑/↓) shows each roll, for example `ATK 7/8`.

## Levels and XP

Echoids level up to **100**. XP needed for the next level is `10 + 4 × level²`:

| Level | XP to next |
|---|---|
| 1 | 14 |
| 5 | 110 |
| 10 | 410 |
| 25 | 2510 |
| 50 | 10010 |
| 75 | 22510 |
| 99 | 39214 |

XP comes from winning and catching ([Battles](Battles.md#xp)) and from Echo Crystals ([Items and Crafting](Items-and-Crafting.md)). Levelling up also restores the HP the echoid gained, and teaches new moves at set levels.

## Names

Every echoid has its own name as well as its species, built by a Markov chain from words themed after its type. Names are 4–9 letters and always the same for the same MAC. Details and examples: [Types → Names](Types.md#names).

## Time of day

Wild echoids only come out at **certain hours**, taken from the Flipper's clock. Each echoid has one or two daily windows, fixed by its MAC, so the same time of day gives the same encounters and a different hour gives others. Rarer echoids are out for less time:

| Rarity | Hours per window | Average hours out per day |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 4–6 | 7.0 |
| Uncommon | 3–5 | 5.7 |
| Rare | 2–4 | 4.3 |
| Epic | 2–3 | 3.6 |
| Legendary | 1–2 | 2.2 |

Access points whose echoid is asleep don't appear in Collect. If nothing is out, Collect says how many echoids are asleep at the current hour. Bluetooth shards and the echoids you own aren't affected.

## Team and PAC

- Your **team** holds up to 6 echoids. The **lead** starts each battle.
- The rest go to **Cold PAC Storage**, 10 echoids per PAC. You start with one PAC and can craft as many more as you like ([Items and Crafting](Items-and-Crafting.md#pacs)).
- An echoid that comes out of the PAC has full HP.
- You can't catch an echoid you already own. The Collect list marks those with `*`.
